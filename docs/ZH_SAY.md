# zh TTS carriers (polyphonic characters)

Generated by `tools/zh_say_scan.py --write`; do not edit by hand. This is a record of the build, not an
ear-check request: the owner cannot verify by ear. It lists which zh words and character units get a `say`
carrier, the text the TTS speaks in place of the written form (docs/PACK_SCHEMA.md words.json).

Safe by construction: every carrier character is monophonic (one reading in pypinyin's heteronym list and in
its phrase data) with the pack's syllable and tone at its position, so the carrier sounds as the pack's pinyin
whatever the TTS's default for the original. Carriers are never displayed, so a rare character is fine when it
is in GB2312 (every zh voice has it) and in jieba's dictionary. Every candidate gets one when it can be built;
confidence (how likely a TTS misread the original, from pypinyin's phrase dictionary) is recorded only.

Unfixable by this mechanism: a neutral-tone syllable (了 le, 呢 ne, 的/地/得 de, 着 zhe, and the second
syllables of 大夫 and 肚子), because no character reads only that neutral syllable; and a syllable whose only
characters are polyphonic (郝 hǎo is also shì in pypinyin, so 好 keeps its written form).

Not covered: the Sounds lessons' `say` strings stay as authored. A lesson item shows its `say` text when no
voice is usable, so a carrier there would be displayed.
