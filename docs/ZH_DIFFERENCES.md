# Behaviour differences vs hsk (intentional, from the extraction)

Moved verbatim from TODO.md on 2026-10-08. Context: packs/zh is ported from the hsk trainer (now ../chinese); these are the deliberate differences.

- **zh UX is pinyin-first again (brief BP, `pack.pronFirst`).** As in hsk, a word shows as pinyin until its character unit is mastered in the 字 stage. A "show written" tap reveals the characters for one item. showChars is replaced by the tiers for good (only the migration's drop list still names it). Per-syllable tone colouring is back (brief BP2, `pack.tones`).
- **zh typed production: two kinds** (`typing: "pron"`, branch engine-zh-typing). The production slot that alternates with recall alternates "Type the pinyin" (silent, tones optional: toneless or other tones count right with a "tones:" note) and "Type the characters" (audio autoplays, IME input). Pinyin is never drilled on its own and there is no typed cloze. Since tones are optional, a sandhi-written pron (一点儿 yìdiǎnr) no longer rejects citation tones. See docs/PACK_SCHEMA.md "Pronunciation aids".
- **Per-word taps inside sentences are back** (brief BP2): each ruby token is a tap that shows the word's popover and speaks the word; the whole sentence is heard from its speaker button.
- **Cloze (gap) items no longer auto-play the sentence** before answering, because hearing it gave the blank away. Audio plays on reveal.
- **The pinyin chart is back as a Sounds "Reference" card** (brief BP2, `pack.soundsReference`), built from the lesson rows, which already cover every initial, final and tone. The Test "Extras" stay dropped: typed pinyin moved into the production slot, and the tone-pattern drill has no replacement.
- **Two function words resolve to their base word.** hsk's function-word list included the compounds 你们 and 他们, which now resolve to their base words 你 and 他. As a result, 你 and 他 are never blanked, including inside 你们 and 他们.
- **`SENTENCE_EXTRA` compounds resolve to their base word's id.** For example, 这个 resolves to 这, which covers 140 of 5040 tokens. They are also listed in `pack.compounds`, so a cloze never blanks 这 inside 这个. `pack_from_hsk.py` also merges adjacent hsk tokens into one longer vocab word, longest match first (8 merges, such as 为+什么 into 为什么).
- **hsk's `hsk_pinyin` progress migrates.** hsk keyed progress by hanzi, the engine by word id; `packs/zh/legacy.json` maps them. At boot, when `vocab_zh` is absent, `migrateLegacy` converts `hsk_pinyin` once, writes `vocab_zh` plus the raw copy under `hsk_pinyin.bak`, and never touches `hsk_pinyin`. Progress import accepts an old hsk export through the same function. `showChars` and `dismissedSoundsHint` are dropped on purpose (docs/HSK_MERGE.md §4, §8).
