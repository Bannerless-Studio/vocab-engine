## p06 · HSK 2 · 了 vs 过 (did vs have ever done)

> 了: an action got done. 过: you have done it before.

## p22 · HSK 4 · 越…越 (the more … the more)

> One thing grows as another grows.  
> 越 A 越 B: 越看越喜欢。

Never offered as a wrong option here: p14.

- 雪[越]下[越]大。 Xuě yuè xià yuè dà. The snow is getting heavier.
- 我[越]看[越]喜欢。 Wǒ yuè kàn yuè xǐhuan. The more I look, the more I like it.
- 他[越]走[越]快。 Tā yuè zǒu yuè kuài. He walks faster and faster.
- 这本书[越]读[越]有意思。 Zhè běn shū yuè dú yuè yǒu yìsi. This book gets better the more I read.
- 她[越]想[越]生气。 Tā yuè xiǎng yuè shēngqì. The more she thought, the angrier she got.
- 他[越]吃[越]胖。 Tā yuè chī yuè pàng. The more he eats, the fatter he gets.

## p23 · HSK 4 · 才 vs 就 (only then (late) vs already (early))

> 就: sooner or easier than expected. 才: later or harder.  
> 六点就起床了 · 十点才起床

Never offered as a wrong option here: p14.

- 他十点[才]起床。 Tā shí diǎn cái qǐchuáng. He didn't get up until ten.
- 我六点[就]起床了。 Wǒ liù diǎn jiù qǐchuáng le. I was up at six already.
- 她十二点[才]回家。 Tā shí'èr diǎn cái huí jiā. She didn't get home until twelve.
- 他五分钟[就]做完了。 Tā wǔ fēnzhōng jiù zuò wán le. He was done in just five minutes.
- 我等了一个小时他[才]来。 Wǒ děng le yī gè xiǎoshí tā cái lái. I waited an hour before he finally came.
- 你怎么现在[才]来？ Nǐ zěnme xiànzài cái lái? Why are you only coming now?
- 我们很快[就]到了。 Wǒmen hěn kuài jiù dào le. We got there in no time.

## p24 · HSK 4 · 难道 (surely … not? (rhetorical))

> A rhetorical question: shows surprise or doubt.  
> 难道 … 吗？

- [难道]你不知道吗？ Nándào nǐ bù zhīdào ma? Don't tell me you don't know!
- [难道]他是你哥哥吗？ Nándào tā shì nǐ gēge ma? Could he be your older brother?
- [难道]你忘记了吗？ Nándào nǐ wàngjì le ma? Did you really forget?
- [难道]这是真的吗？ Nándào zhè shì zhēn de ma? Can this really be true?
- [难道]你不想去吗？ Nándào nǐ bù xiǎng qù ma? Don't you want to go?
- [难道]我错了吗？ Nándào wǒ cuò le ma? Was I really wrong?

## p25 · HSK 4 · 听得懂 / 听不懂 (can / can't (manage to))

> Says whether you can manage a result.  
> verb + 得/不 + result: 听得懂 · 听不懂

- 老师说话很慢，我听[得]懂。 Lǎoshī shuōhuà hěn màn, wǒ tīng de dǒng. The teacher speaks slowly, so I can understand.
- 太快了，我听[不]懂。 Tài kuài le, wǒ tīng bù dǒng. It's too fast, I can't follow.
- 这个字很简单，我看[得]懂。 Zhège zì hěn jiǎndān, wǒ kàn de dǒng. This character is easy; I can read it.
- 太远了，我看[不]清楚。 Tài yuǎn le, wǒ kàn bù qīngchu. It's too far, I can't see clearly.
- 菜太多了，我吃[不]完。 Cài tài duō le, wǒ chī bù wán. There's too much food, I can't finish it.
- 作业不多，我做[得]完。 Zuòyè bù duō, wǒ zuò de wán. There isn't much homework; I can finish it.
- 我找[不]到我的手机。 Wǒ zhǎo bù dào wǒ de shǒujī. I can't find my phone.

## p26 · HSK 4 · 不但…而且 (not only … but also)

> Adds a second, stronger point.  
> 不但 A，而且 B

- 这里[不但]便宜，[而且]很方便。 Zhèlǐ bùdàn piányi, érqiě hěn fāngbiàn. It's not only cheap here but convenient too.
- 她[不但]漂亮，[而且]很聪明。 Tā bùdàn piàoliang, érqiě hěn cōngming. She's not only pretty but smart too.
- 他[不但]会唱歌，[而且]会跳舞。 Tā bùdàn huì chànggē, érqiě huì tiàowǔ. He can not only sing but dance too.
- 这个菜[不但]香，[而且]不贵。 Zhège cài bùdàn xiāng, érqiě bù guì. This dish not only smells good, it's cheap too.
- 我[不但]很累，[而且]很饿。 Wǒ bùdàn hěn lèi, érqiě hěn è. I'm not only tired but hungry too.
- 她[不但]学习好，[而且]身体好。 Tā bùdàn xuéxí hǎo, érqiě shēntǐ hǎo. She not only does well at school but is healthy too.

## p27 · HSK 4 · 除了…以外 (besides, except)

> Besides X (also …), or except X (all …).  
> 除了 X 以外，… 也/还/都 …

- [除了]咖啡[以外]，我也喝茶。 Chúle kāfēi yǐwài, wǒ yě hē chá. Besides coffee, I drink tea too.
- [除了]他[以外]，大家都来了。 Chúle tā yǐwài, dàjiā dōu lái le. Everyone came except him.
- [除了]周末[以外]，我都要上班。 Chúle zhōumò yǐwài, wǒ dōu yào shàngbān. I work every day except weekends.
- [除了]汉语[以外]，你还会什么？ Chúle Hànyǔ yǐwài, nǐ hái huì shénme? Besides Chinese, what else do you know?
- [除了]米饭[以外]，我还想要面条。 Chúle mǐfàn yǐwài, wǒ hái xiǎng yào miàntiáo. Besides rice, I'd also like noodles.
- [除了]我[以外]，没人知道。 Chúle wǒ yǐwài, méi rén zhīdào. Nobody knows except me.
